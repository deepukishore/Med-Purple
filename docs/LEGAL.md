The website exposes `/about`, `/legal`, `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/cookies`,
`/medical-disclaimer`, `/refund-policy`, `/account-deletion`, `/copyright`,
and `/community-guidelines`. About and the legal centre are linked from the
app footer and account screens. Public web pages render without a working API.
The mobile account and profile screens link to equivalent native screens.

Content, founders, revision date, policy navigation, and draft status are shared
in `shared/legal.json`. The confirmed founders are Deepu Kishore and Deepthi.
The pages describe the implemented app, including visible community posts,
planner blocks, flashcard reviews, local focus timer text, and the library-search
coach. No payment flow or AI generation is claimed.

emphasises that app privacy disclosures should accurately describe actual data
practices: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/marketing-your-mobile-app-get-it-right-start

If India is confirmed as the operating jurisdiction, review the official DPDP
Rules and staged commencement against the actual release date before finalising

These references informed review; the pages do not claim certification or
